# Meal plan · 2026-10-18 (week of 19 October 2026)

A week of easy, budget-friendly, heart-healthy home cooking in Prague: less butter, cream and fatty cheese; more beans, lentils, oats, fish and vegetables. Groceries come mostly from **Rohlik**, with one deal from **Albert**. Prices are what the shops listed on 2026-10-18 (\* = on sale that day). They change, so check before buying.

Every item on the shopping list is used in a recipe below. The number in brackets is the dish it's for.

**Kitchen:** gas hob and gas oven, no microwave, a Nutribullet blender, a fridge-freezer.

## The idea: cook once, combine all week

One prep session on Sunday (about 1.5 hours, mostly oven time) makes the building blocks. After that, weeknights take 10–20 minutes.

- Roast chicken + a tray of roasted vegetables
- A pot of quinoa (keeps 4–5 days in the fridge)
- A jar of lemon-yogurt dressing

## Recipes for this week

Amounts are for 2 adults plus leftovers.

### Lemon-yogurt dressing (used all week)
200 g 0% Greek yogurt, juice of 1 lemon, 1 grated garlic clove, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, chopped basil. Keeps 4 days in the fridge.

### #2 Roast chicken + veg tray (Sunday)
1. Rub the chicken with olive oil, salt, paprika and garlic; put half a lemon inside.
2. Roast at 200 °C, about 20 min per 500 g plus 20 min (1.4 kg ≈ 75 min).
3. For the last 35 min, add a second tray: 1 pack sweet potatoes in chunks, 1 broccoli in florets, 2 onions in wedges, with olive oil and salt.
4. Keep half the vegetables for Monday and the leftover chicken for Saturday. Take the skin off on the plate; that's where most of the fat is.
5. Quinoa: 250 g quinoa + 500 ml water, simmer 15 min.

### #1 Lentil-quinoa bowls (Monday, 15 min)
Simmer 100 g red lentils in 300 ml water for 12 min. Bowl: quinoa, lentils, roasted vegetables, baby spinach, half an avocado, dressing, hot sauce.

### #3 Oven salmon (Tuesday, 40 min)
Sweet potato wedges with olive oil at 200 °C for 25 min. Add the salmon (skin down) with olive oil, lemon, garlic and salt for 12–15 min more. Serve with mesclun, cucumber and a spoon of dressing.

### #5 Lighter lasagna (Wednesday, 6–8 portions, freezes)
1. **Sauce:** fry 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves and the mushrooms in a little olive oil. Add the turkey mince and brown it. Add 100 g red lentils, 2 jars Barilla Basilico and 300 ml water. Simmer 20 min.
2. **White layer instead of béchamel:** 400 g cottage cheese + 1 egg + 30 g grated parmesan + a pinch of nutmeg.
3. **Green layer:** 1 bag frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry.
4. Layer sauce, sheets, cottage cheese, spinach; repeat. Top with 2 torn light mozzarellas and 20 g parmesan.
5. Bake at 190 °C: 25 min under foil, then 15 min uncovered.
6. Freeze half in portions. Reheat covered at 180 °C, about 30 min from frozen.

Roughly a third of the saturated fat of a beef-and-béchamel lasagna. Half beef, half turkey also works.

### #10 Light palak paneer (Friday, 30 min)
1. Fry 1 onion in a little oil. Add 3 garlic cloves, a thumb of grated ginger, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp garam masala and chilli to taste.
2. Add the chopped tomatoes and 1 bag frozen spinach. Cook 10 min, then blend smooth in batches.
3. Brown the paneer cubes in 1 tsp oil. Add them to the sauce with 1 can chickpeas (rinsed) and simmer 5 min.
4. Off the heat, stir in 4 tbsp 0% Greek yogurt instead of cream.
5. Serve with basmati.

### #4 Burrito bowls / wraps (Saturday, 15 min)
Warm the leftover chicken and the rinsed black beans. Fill tortillas or bowls with quinoa or rice, beans, chicken, salsa, avocado, a spoon of 0% yogurt instead of sour cream, a little grated cheese and hot sauce.

### B1 Overnight oats (2 min the night before)
75 g oats + 150 g 0% Greek yogurt + a splash of 1.5% milk + a handful of frozen blueberries or a sliced banana, in a jar in the fridge.

### B2 One-minute blender hummus
1 can chickpeas (keep 3 tbsp of the liquid), 1 tbsp almond butter or tahini, juice of ½ lemon, 1 garlic clove, 2 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp cumin, salt. Blend.

### S1 Home popcorn
2 tbsp kernels + 1 tsp oil in a pot with a lid on medium heat; shake when it starts popping. Salt lightly.

### S2 Something sweet
2 squares of 85% dark chocolate, fruit, or dates with almond butter.

## Easy swaps

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| 10% Greek yogurt, sour cream | 0% Greek yogurt |
| Cream cheese or butter on bread | Cottage cheese, hummus, avocado, olive oil |
| Lots of cheddar | Less of it, or Eidam 30%, light mozzarella, a little parmesan |
| Smoked salmon | Fresh or frozen salmon (much less salt) |
| Whole milk | 1.5% milk |
| Snack bars, gummies, popcorn bags | Home popcorn, fruit, homemade granola, dark chocolate |
