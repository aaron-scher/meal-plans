## Easy swaps

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| 10% Greek yogurt, sour cream | 0% Greek yogurt |
| Cream cheese or butter on bread | Cottage cheese, hummus, avocado, olive oil |
| Lots of cheddar | Less of it, or Eidam 30%, light mozzarella, a little parmesan |
| Smoked salmon | Fresh or frozen salmon (much less salt) |
| Whole milk | 1.5% milk |
| Snack bars, gummies, popcorn bags | Home popcorn, fruit, homemade granola, dark chocolate |
