# Shakshuka with spinach and chickpeas

**Dish #13 · serves 2 · 20 min**

Eggs in a spiced tomato sauce. Eat with good bread.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | onion | [Yellow onion, 1 pc](https://www.rohlik.cz/1349783) · Rohlik · 2.56 Kč |
| 2 cloves | garlic | [Garlic, 1 bulb](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350471) · Rohlik · 8.27 Kč |
| 1 tsp | ground cumin | [Heera ground cumin (římský kmín), 100 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1405066) · Rohlik · 79.90 Kč |
| 1 tsp | sweet paprika | [Kotányi sweet paprika, 25 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1311977) · Rohlik · 21.90 Kč |
| 1 jar | Barilla Basilico (or 1 can chopped tomatoes) | [Barilla Basilico tomato sauce, 400 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306279) · Rohlik · 84.90 Kč |
| 2 handfuls | baby spinach | [Baby spinach, 125 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1298747) · Rohlik · 36.90 Kč |
| 1 can | chickpeas, rinsed | [Kitchin chickpeas, 240 g drained](https://www.rohlik.cz/1440196) · Rohlik · 27.90 Kč |
| 4 | eggs | [Schubert eggs, 10 pcs](https://www.rohlik.cz/1394444) · Rohlik · 59.90 Kč |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | [Kitchin extra virgin olive oil, 500 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1484289) · Rohlik · 179.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Fry the onion in the oil 5 min, add garlic, cumin and paprika for 1 min.
2. Add the sauce and chickpeas, simmer 5 min, stir in the spinach.
3. Make 4 hollows and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low 6–8 min, until the whites are set.
