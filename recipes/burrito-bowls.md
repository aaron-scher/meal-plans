# Burrito bowls or wraps

**Dish #4 · serves 2 · 15 min**

Uses the leftover roast chicken. Greek yogurt replaces sour cream.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 2 handfuls | leftover roast chicken | from another recipe |
| 1 can | black beans, rinsed | [Nuevo Progreso black beans, 240 g drained](https://www.rohlik.cz/1421024) · Rohlik · 45.90 Kč |
| 4 | wheat tortillas (or bowls of quinoa or rice) | [Nuevo Progreso wheat tortillas, 8 pcs](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350039) · Rohlik · 29.90 Kč |
| 4 tbsp | salsa | [Poco Loco BIO Acapulco salsa medium, 260 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1356745) · Rohlik · 109.90 Kč |
| 1 | avocado | [Avocado Hass "ready to eat", 4-pack](https://www.rohlik.cz/1422409) · Rohlik · 94.90 Kč |
| 4 tbsp | 0% Greek yogurt | [Milko Greek yogurt 0%, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306505) · Rohlik · 119.90 Kč |
| 20 g | cheddar, grated | [Miil cheddar block, 250 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1406258) · Rohlik · 59.90 Kč |
| ½ | lime | [Lime, 1 pc](https://www.rohlik.cz/1287619) · Rohlik · 13.90 Kč |
| to taste | hot sauce | [Tabasco Sriracha, 256 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1440681) · Rohlik · 164.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Warm the chicken and the rinsed beans in a pan with a pinch of salt and cumin (optional).
2. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan, 20 seconds each side.
3. Fill with beans, chicken, salsa, sliced avocado, a spoon of yogurt, a little cheddar, lime juice and hot sauce.
