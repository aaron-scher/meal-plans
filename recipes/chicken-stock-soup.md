# Chicken stock & soup

**Dish #12 · 2 litres · 2 h, mostly simmering**

Free food from the roast chicken carcass.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | chicken carcass | from another recipe |
| 1 | onion, halved | [Yellow onion, 1 pc](https://www.rohlik.cz/1349783) · Rohlik · 2.56 Kč |
| 3 cloves | garlic | [Garlic, 1 bulb](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350471) · Rohlik · 8.27 Kč |
| 2.5 l | water | from another recipe |
| for soup | leftover chicken, broccoli stalks, a handful of pasta | from another recipe |

## Steps

1. Put the carcass, onion and garlic in a big pot with the water. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently 1.5–2 h.
2. Strain. Cool overnight in the fridge and lift off the solid fat from the top.
3. Use for [cabbage soup](cabbage-soup.md), or heat with leftover chicken, chopped broccoli stalks and a little pasta for a quick soup.
