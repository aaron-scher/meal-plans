# Pasta Basilico with chickpeas and spinach

**Dish #6 · serves 2–3 · 20 min**

A jar-sauce dinner made more filling with beans and greens.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 250 g | wholegrain spaghetti | [Panzani wholegrain spaghetti, 500 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1417473) · Rohlik · 57.90 Kč |
| 1 jar | Barilla Basilico | [Barilla Basilico tomato sauce, 400 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306279) · Rohlik · 84.90 Kč |
| 1 can | chickpeas or cannellini, rinsed | [Kitchin chickpeas, 240 g drained](https://www.rohlik.cz/1440196) · Rohlik · 27.90 Kč |
| 2 handfuls | baby spinach | [Baby spinach, 125 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1298747) · Rohlik · 36.90 Kč |
| 20–30 g | parmesan | [Parmareggio Parmigiano Reggiano, 150 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1325447) · Rohlik · 159.90 Kč |
| 1 clove | garlic | [Garlic, 1 bulb](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350471) · Rohlik · 8.27 Kč |
| 1 tsp | olive oil | [Kitchin extra virgin olive oil, 500 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1484289) · Rohlik · 179.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Cook the spaghetti in salted water.
2. Meanwhile fry the garlic in the olive oil for a minute, add the sauce and the chickpeas, simmer 5 min.
3. Stir in the spinach until it wilts.
4. Mix with the drained pasta. About 10 g parmesan per plate.
