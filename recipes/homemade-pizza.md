# Homemade pizza

**Dish #7 · 1 large pizza · 25 min**

Light mozzarella plus a little parmesan gives plenty of flavour with much less fat than takeaway.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 roll | pizza dough | [Wewalka pizza dough, 280 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1287475) · Rohlik · 41.90 Kč |
| ½ jar | Barilla Basilico (or chopped tomatoes) | [Barilla Basilico tomato sauce, 400 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306279) · Rohlik · 84.90 Kč |
| 1 ball | light mozzarella | [Miil mozzarella light, 125 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1451465) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |
| 10 g | parmesan | [Parmareggio Parmigiano Reggiano, 150 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1325447) · Rohlik · 159.90 Kč |
| 100 g | mushrooms | [Brown mushrooms, 200 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1314129) · Rohlik · 49.90 Kč |
| 1 handful | baby spinach | [Baby spinach, 125 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1298747) · Rohlik · 36.90 Kč |
| ½ | onion | [Yellow onion, 1 pc](https://www.rohlik.cz/1349783) · Rohlik · 2.56 Kč |
| optional | leftover chicken | from another recipe |
| a pinch | chilli | [Kotányi cayenne chilli, 25 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1313167) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |
| 1 tsp | olive oil | [Kitchin extra virgin olive oil, 500 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1484289) · Rohlik · 179.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Heat the oven as hot as it goes (220–250 °C) with the tray inside.
2. Unroll the dough on its paper. Spread the sauce thinly.
3. Add sliced mushrooms, onion, spinach, chicken, torn mozzarella and grated parmesan.
4. Slide onto the hot tray and bake 10–12 min. Finish with olive oil and chilli.
