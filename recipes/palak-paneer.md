# Light palak paneer with chickpeas

**Dish #10 · serves 3 · 30 min**

Half the paneer is swapped for chickpeas, and yogurt replaces cream. Blend the spinach sauce in the Nutribullet.

## Steps

1. Start the rice: rinse, then simmer 250 g in 400 ml water with a pinch of salt, lid on, 12 min. Rest 5 min.
2. Fry the onion in 1 tbsp oil for 5 min. Add garlic, ginger, cumin, turmeric, garam masala and chilli for 1 min.
3. Add the tomatoes and the frozen spinach. Cook 10 min.
4. Blend smooth in the Nutribullet, in two batches. Return to the pan.
5. Brown the paneer in 1 tsp oil in another pan, 3–4 min. Add it to the sauce with the chickpeas. Simmer 5 min.
6. Off the heat, stir in the yogurt. Salt to taste. Serve with the rice.

## Good to know

- The plain Moravia paneer was unavailable on Rohlik in October 2026, so this uses the spiced one.
