# Red lentil dal with spinach

**Dish #11 · 4 portions · 25 min · freezes**

The cheapest meal here, about 15 Kč a portion.

## Steps

1. Fry the onion in the oil 5 min. Add garlic, ginger, turmeric and cumin for 1 min.
2. Add the rinsed lentils and 900 ml water. Simmer 20 min, stirring, until thick.
3. Stir in the spinach and lemon juice. Salt to taste.
4. Serve with rice or quinoa and a spoon of yogurt.
