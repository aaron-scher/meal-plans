# Roast chicken + veg tray

**Dish #2 · serves 4 (with leftovers) · 1 h 30 min, mostly oven**

The Sunday prep dish. The leftover chicken and vegetables become bowls and burritos later in the week.

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Rub the chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika and 2 crushed garlic cloves. Put half a lemon inside.
3. Roast about 20 min per 500 g plus 20 min (1.4 kg ≈ 75 min). It's done when the juices run clear.
4. For the last 35 min, add a second tray: sweet potato chunks, broccoli florets and onion wedges with 2 tbsp olive oil and salt.
5. Let the chicken rest 10 min before carving. Take the skin off on the plate; that's where most of the fat is.
6. Keep half the vegetables and the leftover meat in the fridge for the week. Keep the carcass for [chicken stock](chicken-stock-soup.md).
