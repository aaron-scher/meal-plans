# Lemon-yogurt dressing

**Makes 1 jar · 5 min · keeps 4 days**

The sauce for bowls, salads, salmon and wraps. Made with 0% Greek yogurt, so it's creamy without the fat.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 200 g | 0% Greek yogurt | [Milko Greek yogurt 0%, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306505) · Rohlik · 119.90 Kč |
| 1 | lemon (juice) | [Lemons, 500 g net](https://www.rohlik.cz/1331029) · Rohlik · 39.90 Kč |
| 1 clove | garlic, grated | [Garlic, 1 bulb](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350471) · Rohlik · 8.27 Kč |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil | [Kitchin extra virgin olive oil, 500 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1484289) · Rohlik · 179.90 Kč |
| a few leaves | basil, chopped | [Basil in a pot](https://www.rohlik.cz/1309415) · Rohlik · 49.90 Kč |
| to taste | salt and pepper | [Bad Ischler salt, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1456570) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Mix everything in a jar.
2. Taste and add more lemon or salt if needed.
3. Keep in the fridge.
