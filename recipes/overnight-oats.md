# Overnight oats

**1 jar · 2 min the night before**

The easiest breakfast there is, and oats are one of the best foods for cholesterol.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 75 g | oat flakes | [Yutto fine oat flakes, 500 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1454580) · Rohlik · 11.90 Kč |
| 150 g | 0% Greek yogurt | [Milko Greek yogurt 0%, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306505) · Rohlik · 119.90 Kč |
| a splash | 1.5% milk | [Miil milk 1.5%, 1 l](https://www.rohlik.cz/1441840) · Rohlik · 13.90 Kč |
| a handful | frozen blueberries | [Agro Jesenice frozen wild blueberries, 200 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1479673) · Rohlik · 59.90 Kč |
| ½ | banana, sliced (or instead of the berries) | [Bananas, ~0.65 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1375865) · Rohlik · 32.44 Kč |
| 1 tsp (optional) | honey | [Medokomerc flower honey, 350 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1455768) · Rohlik · 109.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Put the oats, yogurt and milk in a jar and stir.
2. Add the frozen blueberries or banana on top.
3. Close the jar and leave it in the fridge overnight. Eat cold.

## Good to know

- Make 2–3 jars at once; they keep 3 days.
- Cinnamon or a spoon of almond butter on top is good too.
