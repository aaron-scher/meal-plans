# Lentil-quinoa bowls

**Dish #1 · serves 2 · 15 min**

Our usual, with 0% yogurt in the dressing. Fastest when the quinoa and roasted veg are already made.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 250 g | quinoa (cooks to 4–5 portions) | [Grizly white quinoa, 500 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1462576) · Rohlik · 95.90 Kč |
| 100 g | red lentils | [Kitchin red lentils, 500 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1455387) · Rohlik · 33.90 Kč |
| 2 handfuls | roasted vegetables | [Czech sweet potatoes, 500 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1424994) · Rohlik · 59.90 Kč |
| 2 handfuls | baby spinach | [Baby spinach, 125 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1298747) · Rohlik · 36.90 Kč |
| 1 | avocado | [Avocado Hass "ready to eat", 4-pack](https://www.rohlik.cz/1422409) · Rohlik · 94.90 Kč |
| 4 tbsp | lemon-yogurt dressing | from another recipe |
| to taste | hot sauce | [Tabasco Sriracha, 256 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1440681) · Rohlik · 164.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Quinoa (if not made yet): rinse 250 g quinoa, simmer in 500 ml water for 15 min, let it stand 5 min.
2. Simmer the red lentils in 300 ml water for 12 min until soft. Salt lightly.
3. Fill bowls with quinoa, lentils, roasted vegetables (from the [veg tray](roast-chicken-veg-tray.md)) and spinach.
4. Top with sliced avocado, [lemon-yogurt dressing](lemon-yogurt-dressing.md) and hot sauce.
