# Stovetop popcorn

**1 big bowl · 5 min**

Much cheaper than bags of popcorn, and you control the salt.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 3 tbsp | popcorn kernels | [Alnatura BIO popcorn kernels, 500 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1400740) · Rohlik · 78.90 Kč |
| 1 tsp | rapeseed oil | [Kitchin rapeseed oil, 1 l](https://www.rohlik.cz/1462232) · Rohlik · 36.90 Kč |
| a pinch | salt | [Bad Ischler salt, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1456570) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Put the oil and kernels in a pot with a lid on medium heat.
2. When it starts popping, shake the pot every few seconds.
3. When the popping slows to a few seconds apart, take it off the heat. Salt lightly.
