# Cabbage soup, upgraded

**Dish #8 · 6 portions · 50 min · freezes**

More flavour and a full meal: spices fried first, chicken stock, beans and potato.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| ½ head | white cabbage, shredded | [White cabbage, 1 head (~1.9 kg)](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350891) · Rohlik · 38.27 Kč |
| 1 | onion | [Yellow onion, 1 pc](https://www.rohlik.cz/1349783) · Rohlik · 2.56 Kč |
| 3 cloves | garlic | [Garlic, 1 bulb](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350471) · Rohlik · 8.27 Kč |
| 1 tsp | sweet paprika (smoked if you have it) | [Kotányi sweet paprika, 25 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1311977) · Rohlik · 21.90 Kč |
| 1 tsp | caraway seeds | [Kotányi caraway seeds (kmín celý), 28 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1293571) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |

| 1 | lemon | [Lemons, 500 g net](https://www.rohlik.cz/1331029) · Rohlik · 39.90 Kč |

| to serve | 0% Greek yogurt | [Milko Greek yogurt 0%, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306505) · Rohlik · 119.90 Kč |
| to serve | fresh dill | [Titbit fresh dill, 30 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1309951) · Rohlik · 29.90 Kč |
| to taste | salt and pepper | [Bad Ischler salt, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1456570) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Fry the onion in the oil for 5 min. Add garlic, paprika and caraway for 1 min.
2. Add the cabbage and stir for 3 min.
3. Add the stock (from [chicken stock](chicken-stock-soup.md)) and potatoes. Simmer 25 min.
4. Add the beans and simmer 5 min more. Season with salt, pepper and lemon juice.
5. Serve with a spoon of yogurt and dill.
