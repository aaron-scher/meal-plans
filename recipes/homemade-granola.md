# Homemade granola

**1 big jar · 30 min · keeps 2 weeks**

About half the sugar of shop granola. On 0% yogurt with berries.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 300 g | oat flakes | [Yutto fine oat flakes, 500 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1454580) · Rohlik · 11.90 Kč |
| 100 g | walnuts, chopped | [Yutto walnuts, 200 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1436383) · Rohlik · 74.90 Kč |
| 3 tbsp | almond butter | [Vilgain 100% almond butter](https://vilgain.cz/vilgain-mandlove-maslo) · Vilgain |
| 3 tbsp | honey | [Medokomerc flower honey, 350 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1455768) · Rohlik · 109.90 Kč |
| 1 tsp | cinnamon | [Allnature organic cinnamon, 30 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1464882) · Rohlik · 30.90 Kč |
| a pinch | salt | [Bad Ischler salt, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1456570) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |
| handful (optional) | dates, chopped, added after baking | [Alnatura BIO dates, 200 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1441791) · Rohlik · 82.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 160 °C.
2. Warm the almond butter and honey for a moment so they mix easily. Stir into the oats, nuts, cinnamon and salt.
3. Spread on a baking tray. Bake 20–25 min, stirring halfway.
4. Let it cool on the tray (it gets crunchy), then add the dates. Store in a jar.
