# Oven salmon with sweet potato

**Dish #3 · serves 2 · 40 min**

Salmon is a good fat. Fresh or frozen beats smoked, which is very salty.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 400 g | salmon fillet with skin | [Salmon fillet with skin, duo 400 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1311671) · Rohlik · 319.90 Kč |

| to taste | salt and pepper | [Bad Ischler salt, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1456570) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cut the sweet potatoes into wedges, toss with 1 tbsp olive oil and salt, roast 25 min.
2. Put the salmon skin-down on the same tray. Drizzle with olive oil, lemon juice, grated garlic, salt and pepper.
3. Roast 12–15 min more, until the salmon flakes.
4. Serve with mesclun and cucumber, and a spoon of [lemon-yogurt dressing](lemon-yogurt-dressing.md).
