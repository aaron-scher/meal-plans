# Better nachos

**Dish #9 · serves 2 · 15 min**

Beans, chicken and salsa do the work; a little cheese for flavour.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 2 handfuls | tortilla chips | [Nuevo Progreso tortilla chips, 400 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1421146) · Rohlik · 99.90 Kč |
| 1 can | black beans, rinsed | [Nuevo Progreso black beans, 240 g drained](https://www.rohlik.cz/1421024) · Rohlik · 45.90 Kč |
| 1 handful | leftover chicken | from another recipe |
| 4 tbsp | salsa | [Poco Loco BIO Acapulco salsa medium, 260 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1356745) · Rohlik · 109.90 Kč |
| 30 g | cheddar, grated | [Miil cheddar block, 250 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1406258) · Rohlik · 59.90 Kč |
| 4 tbsp | 0% Greek yogurt | [Milko Greek yogurt 0%, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1306505) · Rohlik · 119.90 Kč |
| 1 | avocado | [Avocado Hass "ready to eat", 4-pack](https://www.rohlik.cz/1422409) · Rohlik · 94.90 Kč |
| to taste | hot sauce | [Tabasco Sriracha, 256 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1440681) · Rohlik · 164.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Spread the chips on a tray.
2. Top with beans, chicken, spoons of salsa and the cheddar.
3. Bake 8 min. Top with yogurt, avocado and hot sauce.
