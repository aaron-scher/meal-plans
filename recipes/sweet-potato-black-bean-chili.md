# Sweet potato & black bean chili

**Dish #14 · 5 portions · 45 min · freezes**

A big pot that gets better the next day.

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |

| 1 tsp | sweet paprika | [Kotányi sweet paprika, 25 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1311977) · Rohlik · 21.90 Kč |

| to serve | lime | [Lime, 1 pc](https://www.rohlik.cz/1287619) · Rohlik · 13.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Fry the onion in the oil 5 min (with the turkey, if using, until browned). Add garlic and spices for 1 min.
2. Add the sweet potato, beans, lentils, tomatoes and 500 ml water.
3. Simmer 30 min, stirring now and then, until the sweet potato is soft. Salt to taste.
4. Serve with yogurt, avocado and lime.
