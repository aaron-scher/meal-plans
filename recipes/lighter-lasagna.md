# Lighter lasagna

**Dish #5 · 6–8 portions · 1 h 15 min · freezes**

Turkey and red lentils in the sauce, cottage cheese instead of béchamel, light mozzarella on top. About a third of the saturated fat of a classic lasagna. Half beef, half turkey also works.

## Steps

1. Thaw the spinach (in a sieve, or the night before in the fridge) and squeeze out the water.
2. Sauce: fry the onion, garlic and mushrooms in the olive oil for 5 min. Add the turkey and brown it, breaking it up.
3. Add the red lentils, both jars of Barilla and 300 ml water. Simmer 20 min, stirring now and then.
4. White layer: mix the cottage cheese, egg, 30 g parmesan and nutmeg.
5. Heat the oven to 190 °C. In a baking dish, layer: sauce, sheets, cottage cheese mix, spinach. Repeat, ending with sauce.
6. Top with the torn mozzarella and 20 g parmesan. Cover with foil.
7. Bake 25 min covered, then 15 min uncovered. Rest 10 min before cutting.
8. Freeze half in portions. Reheat covered at 180 °C, about 30 min from frozen.
