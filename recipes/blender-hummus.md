# One-minute blender hummus

**1 bowl · 1 min · keeps 4 days**

For crispbread, vegetables and wraps.

## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient | Where to buy |
|---|---|---|
| 1 can | chickpeas (keep 3 tbsp of the liquid) | [Kitchin chickpeas, 240 g drained](https://www.rohlik.cz/1440196) · Rohlik · 27.90 Kč |
| 1 tbsp | almond butter or tahini | [Vilgain 100% almond butter](https://vilgain.cz/vilgain-mandlove-maslo) · Vilgain |
| ½ | lemon (juice) | [Lemons, 500 g net](https://www.rohlik.cz/1331029) · Rohlik · 39.90 Kč |
| 1 clove | garlic | [Garlic, 1 bulb](https://www.rohlik.cz/1350471) · Rohlik · 8.27 Kč |
| 2 tbsp | olive oil | [Kitchin extra virgin olive oil, 500 ml](https://www.rohlik.cz/1484289) · Rohlik · 179.90 Kč |
| ½ tsp | ground cumin | [Heera ground cumin (římský kmín), 100 g](https://www.rohlik.cz/1405066) · Rohlik · 79.90 Kč |
| a pinch | salt | [Bad Ischler salt, 1 kg](https://www.rohlik.cz/1456570) · Rohlik · 22.90 Kč |

## Steps

1. Put everything in the Nutribullet, including the 3 tbsp chickpea liquid.
2. Blend until smooth, about 30 seconds. Add a little water if it's too thick.

## Good to know

- Tahini gives the classic taste: [Natural Jihlava tahini](https://www.rohlik.cz/1470453) on Rohlik. Almond butter works too.
